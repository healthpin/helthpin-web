# Health Pin Web Console

## Scope

Visitor mode: Operate. This brief covers the full role-aware web console: shared authentication, the super-admin area, and the hospital workspace across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Audience: Health Pin super-admins and hospital operations staff. Job: scan current operational state, find a record quickly, and complete account, directory, booking, doctor, queue, prescription, or settings work without losing context. Existing application behavior, APIs, permissions, routes, validation, and content remain untouched.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Health Pin behaves like one precise care registry with a current record always visible. It refuses the interchangeable metric-card dashboard by making hierarchy, active context, and record comparison the memorable system.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep logo berry forms the navigation spine and primary actions; warm ivory is the working ground; white holds records; champagne gold marks the current path. Plum ink, fine plum rules, 12–16px radii, and restrained berry-tinted shadows keep dense work calm.

**STORY:** Users immediately know which Health Pin role and workspace they occupy, scan the day's live state, enter a module, compare consistently ordered records, act, and receive unambiguous feedback without a visual reset between pages.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact berry sidebar with a moving gold active marker frames a white header and warm workspace. The page title and primary action lead; summary records form one bordered group rather than floating tiles; live work, filters, and tables follow in aligned, responsive regions.

**FORM:** Care Record Tabs, third in the grounded direction list. Signature interaction: the champagne current-record marker travels from global navigation to local tabs and selected rows while standard web controls remain familiar. seed:e9f41f8a5a0114d1.
